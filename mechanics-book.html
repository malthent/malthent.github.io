About the book

An introductory mechanics textbook that combines analytical and numerical methods from the first chapter. Solving the equations of motion numerically lets students work on realistic problems early: motion with air resistance, measured force curves, and data from real experiments, alongside the classical exactly solvable cases.

The book covers motion and forces in one, two and three dimensions, constrained motion, work and energy, momentum and collisions, multiparticle systems, and the rotation and dynamics of rigid bodies. It includes a chapter on the basics of scientific programming in Python. A parallel edition, Elementary Mechanics Using Matlab, uses Matlab instead.
