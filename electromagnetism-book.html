About the book

An introductory electromagnetism textbook with computation built into the explanations, examples and exercises. Worked examples follow a whole problem: from building a model, to simplifying it into a form that can be solved analytically, to solving the full problem numerically.

The book runs from the electric field and potential through Gauss' law, dielectrics and ideal conductors to currents and circuits, magnetic fields, Faraday's law, inductance and Maxwell's equations. Numerical integration and numerical solutions of Laplace's and Poisson's equations are used throughout.
